# Welcome to voce

voce reads Markdown aloud. Each sentence is marked as it is read, and each word as it is said, so you can follow
along or look away and just listen.

Tap any sentence to go there. Tap the one being read to pause. Your place is saved as you go, so you can stop on
your phone and pick up on your laptop where you left off.

## Two voices

The voice your device has is instant, and fine for most writing. Kokoro is a voice that runs inside the page, on
your device. It takes a minute to download once, then reads code and names more evenly. Choose one in settings.

Nothing you listen to is sent anywhere to be spoken. Your library lives in this repository, on your GitHub account,
and nowhere else.
