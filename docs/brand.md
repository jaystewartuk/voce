# The voce brand

## Name

**voce** is Italian for "voice", said *VOH-chay*. It sits in the family of musical directions such as *sotto
voce* (in an undertone) and *viva voce* (aloud). It is always written in lower case, like the command.

## Mark

The mark shows what voce does. Three lines of text, where the middle one carries the pale band of the sentence
being read and, inside it, the deep blue of the word being spoken. Sound waves come off the right-hand side.

Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width. Don't recolour it, stretch it or put it on a
busy background.

## Colour

The palette comes straight from the reader, so the brand and the product look the same.

| Name | Hex | xterm-256 | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0E1116` | — | backgrounds, the mark's tile, text on light |
| Paper | `#F6F3EE` | — | light backgrounds |
| Voce blue | `#2F6FEB` | 25 / 33 | the spoken word, headings, links, accents |
| Sentence | `#AFD7FF` | 153 | the band behind the sentence being read |
| Slate | `#3B4452` | — | quiet lines in the mark, borders |
| Mist | `#8B949E` | 245 | secondary text |

In the terminal, the spoken word is white on xterm colour 25, the sentence is black on 153, and headings are 33.

## Type

Code and the wordmark use the system monospace: SF Mono, Menlo or Consolas. The website sets prose in the system
sans serif. No web fonts are loaded, so nothing is fetched from anyone else's servers.

## Voice

Plain and direct, the way voce reads: short sentences, one idea each, no hype. Say what it does and how to do it.
